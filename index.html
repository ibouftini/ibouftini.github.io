About Me

PhD Student @ CEA Grenoble

  • Data Science
  • Signal Processing
  • Applied Math
  • Battery Intelligence
  • Medical Imaging

I’m a PhD student at CEA Grenoble, working on battery ageing: making Li-ion and Na-ion batteries last longer and run more safely. I build machine learning models that learn from real internal and external measurements and stay consistent with the physics of how cells degrade.

Before this, I studied data science, signal processing and applied mathematics at Centrale Nantes, and spent two internships applying deep learning to medical imaging, at GE HealthCare and Ai Movement.
