Research and Industry

2026 - Present
PhD Student, Machine Learning for Battery Ageing
@ CEA Grenoble

Doctoral research on degradation monitoring for lithium-ion and sodium-ion batteries. Enrolled at Université Grenoble Alpes (EEATS doctoral school) and co-supervised with Sorbonne Université.

  • Develop physics-informed deep learning models that build knowledge of cell ageing into the training, so that predictions stay consistent with the underlying electrochemistry.
  • Combine several kinds of measurement: signals from embedded optical fibre sensors, reference electrodes, X-ray characterization and electron microscopy.
  • Use these models to identify which degradation mechanisms are active, estimate state of health (SOH) and predict remaining useful life (RUL).
Physics-informed ML Battery ageing Multimodal data
Apr - Sep 2026
Deep Learning Research Intern (Master's Thesis Internship)
@ GE HealthCare

Master's thesis internship on deep learning for ischemic stroke assessment from 4D CT perfusion (CTP) scans.

  • Built an end-to-end deep learning pipeline that segments the ischemic core and penumbra, to replace a classical deconvolution method that fails on edge cases and needs manual input from the clinician.
  • Reached a mean Dice of 0.63 against 0.40 for 4DMJNET, the learned reference model, and cut inference to about 15 s per case on GPU, against 1 to 2 min for the deconvolution pipeline.
  • Assembled the evaluation data from 3 public datasets, compared the results with 3 clinical software tools (RAPID, icobrain cva, CTP4D) and ran ablation studies to justify each architectural choice.
  • Ported the full inference pipeline to C++ with OpenVINO (file reading, preprocessing, forward pass, post-processing) and built a Flask web app that displays the results for clinicians.
Segmentation CT perfusion C++ / OpenVINO
Apr - Aug 2025
Data Science Research Intern, Breast Cancer Detection
@ Ai Movement

Graph neural networks for breast cancer detection from multi-view mammograms.

  • Implemented the multi-view architecture of “Act Like a Radiologist” (AGN) in PyTorch from the paper alone, with no reference code, and reproduced its full detection pipeline on scarce clinical data.
  • Redesigned the attention gates to output weights in [−1, 1] instead of [0, 1], so that a gate can strengthen a feature as well as keep or weaken it, and added residual connections to improve gradient flow.
  • Stabilized training with a two-stage strategy: Mask R-CNN pretraining, then AGN fine-tuning.
  • Outperformed the Mask R-CNN, DETR and YOLOv8 baselines by 9.5 percentage points in Recall@0.5FPI (the share of tumours detected at 0.5 false positives per image).
Graph neural networks Mammography Object detection

Research Projects

LS2N Lab, Centrale Nantes
Physics-Informed Neural Network for Fuel Cell Health Prognosis
Supervised by Mickaël Hilairet
  • Designed a degradation model of a hydrogen fuel cell that encodes its electrochemical dynamics as physics constraints in the loss function of a neural network (PINN).
  • Estimated remaining useful life with 24% lower prediction error than a Kalman filter baseline.
  • Validated the model on long-term experimental degradation trajectories from several operating scenarios.
Physics-informed ML Prognostics Fuel cells
LS2N Lab, Centrale Nantes
Sparse Signal Reconstruction for Ultrasonic Non-Destructive Testing
Supervised by Sébastien Bourguignon
  • Recovered sparse reflectivity from noisy ultrasound measurements by ℓ1-regularized deconvolution solved with ISTA.
  • Implemented proximal gradient steps with the spectral norm estimated by power iteration and FFT-based convolution in O(N log N).
  • Detected echo peaks down to 10 dB SNR, where least squares failed at 40 dB, and cut RMSE by 65% with the best regularization weight.
  • Measured the thickness of metal plates to ±0.3 mm from raw ultrasound signals.
Inverse problems Sparse optimization Ultrasound
